About Apex

Built around real understanding

We started Apex with a simple belief: every student can love learning when they have the right instructor, the right pace, and the right amount of attention.

Our Story

From one classroom to a full center

Apex began as a handful of instructors tutoring students out of a small Beirut classroom. Word spread quickly — not because of marketing, but because students' grades and confidence genuinely improved. Today, Apex connects students across mathematics, sciences, literature, and languages with instructors who are just as invested in understanding as in results.

Whether online or in person, every session is built around the student in front of us, not a fixed curriculum.
